# The shelf of devices

Things a page can be built around. Take what serves the concept, combine two, or invent a
neighbour. A page built on one or two of these, done properly, beats a page that samples ten.

## Opening

- A split with the picture larger than the words, or the words larger than the picture.
- A poster: type only, set at a size that needs no image.
- Letters as a window onto something moving behind them.
- A scene that stays put while the first section slides over it.
- One object, lit, turning or drifting, with nothing else on stage.
- A field generated in code that reacts to the pointer or to time.

## Finding the way

- A floating bar that shrinks to a pill once the page moves.
- A menu that opens as a full sheet, its items arriving one after another.
- A rail of section names that follows the scroll and marks where you are.
- A dock of icons that swell under the pointer.

## Arranging

- A tile grid of uneven sizes, some tiles alive.
- A wall with no fixed row height.
- Two halves that travel in opposite directions.
- Rows that alternate which side the picture is on, broken before the third.
- A horizontal strip that takes over the vertical scroll for a while.

## Holding things

- A card that tips toward the pointer.
- A card whose edge lights where the pointer is.
- A pane of glass with a bright rim.
- A stack you can flick through.
- A button that grows into the panel it opens.

## Moving with the scroll

- Cards that pin at the top and pile up.
- A line or a route that draws itself as far as you have read.
- A picture that grows from small to full as it arrives.
- A sequence of frames tied to the scroll position.
- Layers moving at different speeds.

## Showing pictures

- A strip of narrow slices; the one under the pointer opens.
- A fan of cards with the middle one facing you.
- A surface you can drag in any direction.
- A trail of images left behind the pointer.

## Letters

- A band of words running sideways, faster when you scroll.
- Characters that settle from noise into the word.
- Text set around a circle, turning slowly.
- A headline whose weight or width changes as it arrives.
- An outline that fills in.

## Small responses

- A button that leans toward the pointer and presses in.
- A fill that enters from the side the pointer came from.
- A ripple from the point of a click.
- A shimmer crossing a placeholder.
- A number that counts up once.
- A colour field that shifts like slow weather.
