# The menu: what can be asked of kubik

Show this, shortened to what fits the moment, when the person asks what kubik can do, or gave no
direction and is there to choose. It is also the list a wish is matched against: anything named
here can be asked for by name, and settles that card instead of leaving it to the dice.

A call can be as short as the kind, or as full as the person likes:

```
kubik: slides about the quarter
kubik: a report, quiet, like a newspaper, green
kubik: landing for the plugin, loud, dark, no purple
kubik: figures for the board, our brand blue, with a pie of the cost split
kubik: a design system for the product, calm, like a precision instrument
kubik: a page like this one (a link or a screenshot), but ours
```

## Five kinds

| Kind | For |
|---|---|
| a page | a landing page, a marketing site, a portfolio, an editorial page, a redesign |
| a report | findings, a status, an audit: a document one reader decides from |
| slides | a talk, a pitch, a walkthrough, shown on a stage or read on a phone |
| figures | a dashboard, a KPI summary, a board pack: charts, pies, tables, big numbers |
| a system | a design system and UI kit for a back office, a console, a management system: the record (`DESIGN.md`), the tokens, a working specimen |

## Three registers, for every kind

| Register | Ask with | You get |
|---|---|---|
| quiet | clean, flat, simple, calm, strict | flat colour, one typeface, one accent, one drawn detail; nothing loops |
| balanced | nothing | the kind as its file describes it |
| loud | bold, striking, experimental, "wow" | depth, atmosphere and motion as part of the idea |

## Four page styles

| Style | Ask with | It looks like |
|---|---|---|
| minimal | minimal, editorial, "like a document" | warm paper, one serif, hairlines, a single rare colour |
| brutal | brutalist, industrial, blueprint, terminal | a printed technical sheet or a live terminal |
| luxe | premium, high-end, luxury | machined surfaces, light from somewhere, slow weight |
| cinema | cinematic, scroll-driven, like a film | four acts, set pieces, the scroll as the camera |

A page with no style named sets three dials itself: `nerve` (how far the composition goes),
`motion`, `density`. They can be given as numbers from 1 to 10.

## Worlds, by kind

Each can be asked for by name. Left unasked, the dice choose.

- **A report:** a field report; a broadsheet front page; a lab notebook; an annual report; a
  dossier with tabs and stamps; a Swiss engineering memo; an atlas with plates; a zine.
- **Slides:** a blueprint; keynote black; a Swiss poster; a chalkboard; a whiteboard sketch; cut
  paper; a magazine spread; a museum label; a terminal session; comic panels; a night sky.
- **Figures:** an annual report; a consulting page; a control room; the business page of a daily;
  a trading terminal; a factory board; a bank statement; an investor letter.
- **A system**, for software people work in: an instrument panel; a ledger; a clean office; a
  public service; a workshop; a clinic; a control desk. Its layout stays the familiar one; the
  dice choose its character. A system for a brand's site is looser, and is made only when asked.
- **A page** has colour families instead of worlds: cold luxury; forest, bone and amber; black and
  tan; cobalt and cream; terracotta and slate; olive, brick and paper; monochrome with one pop;
  ink and acid; dusk; candy on white.

## What else a wish can settle

A colour or a brand; a typeface; a reference ("like this site": kubik takes its decisions, not its
pixels, and says what it took); a `DESIGN.md` already in the project, which settles everything it
covers; the audience; what must not
appear ("no purple", "no stock photos"); the chart a number should be shown as; how many slides;
the language.

Anything not on this menu can still be asked for. The shelves are examples, and kubik is expected
to invent in the same spirit.
